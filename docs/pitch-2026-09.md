The faster decisions get made, the more it's worth keeping track of the "why" behind them.  
  
As agents accelerate business, Longreach builds organizational memory with an economic purpose. We provide a system of record for a new kind of first-class data object. Longreach captures assumptions and causal reasoning behind important decisions, and helps keep your commitments in line with changing evidence. We price reconsideration and give you options while they're still economically viable. 

Your organization has three different clocks: evidence changes, commitments persist, and options expire. A single budget number conceals those differences. When evidence for an assumption changes, the organization can immediately discover which commitments should care, and by how much.  
  
An example: let's say your analyst predicts rain next week, and recommends that you buy 5,000 shares of [umbrellas.com](http://umbrellas.com/). That's an investment thesis, which can be captured as a *what* (buy X shares of Y) and a *why* (because it's likely to rain). We already capture the what in a financial ledger, a system of record. Your position is not stochastic, you can look up precisely how many shares you hold, along with all the details and trends of price and timing. Portfolio analysis already gives plenty of quant analysis of your *what*.


Longreach provides, for the first time, a ledger or system of record for the *why*. Since it's deterministic and not a black box that burns tokens erratically, it provides a more solid foundation for reasoning and quant analytics. You can, for example, measure the gap between a stated assumption/prediction (it's likely to rain) and evidence (skies are clearing) as conditions change. You can put a price on reconsidering your investments, as the gaps change over time. You have options, and like Black-Scholes, you can price the value of exercising those options while they still have economic value.


We’re a new capital-allocation capability/ontology that gives you the power to

- Know which commitments have stopped earning the right to consume tomorrow’s resources.  
    Longreach continuously tests the assumptions that justified yesterday’s commitments against today’s evidence. Stop funding decisions after the facts that earned them have changed.
    
- Compute what changing your mind is still worth.  
    Every commitment contains a shrinking window of economically useful reconsideration. Longreach identifies what is still governable, what alternatives remain available, what changing course costs—and what waiting costs. Optionality stops being abstract; it becomes an economic object. We change the economics of reconsideration.
    
- Turn your ‘whys' into executable enterprise assets  
    Most systems remember what was decided. Longreach makes the causal logic behind consequential commitments computationally addressable: We authorized X because we believed A, B and C. When B changes, the organization can immediately discover which commitments should care, and by how much.
    
- Make one changed fact productive everywhere it matters.  
    A new competitor, demand signal, regulatory change or technology breakthrough shouldn’t have to be rediscovered meeting by meeting, budget by budget, investment by investment. Longreach propagates changed evidence through the commitments that depend upon it.
    
- Discover the options hiding inside commitments you already made.  
    Changed assumptions do not necessarily mean stop. They can mean redirect, resize, renegotiate, repurpose, accelerate, combine or sell. Longreach turns reconsideration from a kill mechanism into an option-discovery mechanism for capital, capabilities and relationships already assembled.
    
- Pragmatically price organizational delay  
    “We should revisit this” is economically meaningless without when. Longreach makes visible the capital still governable and the optionality decaying while nobody acts. The question becomes not merely Should we reconsider? but _What does another 30 days of not reconsidering cost us?_
    
- Route changed evidence to someone who can actually do something about it.  
    Evidence and authority operate on different clocks. Longreach connects the diff — what materially changed — to the decision rights required to respond while response still has economic value. Insight without authority is just another notification.
    
- Learn which reasons actually deserve your money.  
    Across hundreds of commitments, Longreach creates something individual decision reviews cannot: a learning surface. Which assumptions repeatedly survive? Which fail? Which signals predict successful reconsideration? Which leaders change course too early, too late, or never? The organization begins learning from cohorts of decisions, not merely remembering individual ones.
    
- Compound your ability to allocate capital intelligently.  
    Every commitment makes the next commitment more informed. Longreach preserves the relationship between reason → commitment → changed evidence → reconsideration → action → outcome.Over time, the enterprise develops proprietary evidence about when its own judgment deserves confidence—and when it deserves challenge.
